Safety checklist

Most crypto losses start before the transaction.

Identity spoofing, malicious approvals, fake support and social-engineering attacks can look legitimate. Slow verification is a feature.

Before receiving AEDT

  • Confirm TRON / TRC-20.
  • Compare the full contract address.
  • Test with a small transfer when appropriate.
  • Keep wallet software updated.

Before swapping AEDT

  • Check pool identity and available liquidity.
  • Review expected price impact and slippage.
  • Do not assume website reference value equals executable market price.
  • Use independent explorers after the transaction.
