Transparency

Transparency means separating fact from positioning.

AEDT uses AED-oriented branding and market positioning, but public-market price and liquidity can vary. This page makes those boundaries explicit.

Fact

Public contract

The AEDT TRC-20 contract is publicly inspectable on TRON and should be used as the primary identity reference.

Positioning

AED-linked narrative

“AED-linked” describes the project’s intended value and utility framing. It does not by itself establish a legal redemption right or guaranteed market peg.
