Legal & risk

Legal, risk & website disclosures.

Plain-language boundaries for how AEDT information should be understood. This template should be reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions where the project operates or markets.

Informational website

This website is intended to provide project, technical, market-reference and ecosystem information. It is not individualized investment, legal, tax or financial advice.

No affiliation claim

AEDT is presented as an independent project and is not affiliated with Tether or USDT. References to a USDT trading pair describe a market pair only.

No guaranteed value or redemption

References to AED or AED-linked positioning do not guarantee that AEDT will trade at a fixed AED value. Unless separate binding terms expressly state otherwise, website language does not create a guaranteed redemption obligation.

Third-party data

Market and blockchain information may be sourced from public third-party services. Data can be delayed, incorrect, unavailable or inconsistent. Always verify through independent sources.

Digital-asset risks

Digital assets can involve market, liquidity, technology, smart-contract, cyber-security, operational, fraud, tax, legal and regulatory risks. Transactions can be irreversible.

Jurisdiction and compliance

Availability and permitted use of digital assets varies by jurisdiction. Businesses and users are responsible for understanding applicable laws. Regulated services should be undertaken with appropriately licensed or qualified professionals.

Website security

The website should never request a seed phrase or private key. Links to third parties are provided for convenience and independent verification; third-party sites operate under their own terms and security controls.

Forward-looking roadmap

Roadmap items, planned integrations and future features are goals, not guarantees. Timing, scope and availability can change.

Professional review

This page is a product-copy and website-disclosure template, not a legal opinion. Before public launch or major distribution, the project should obtain jurisdiction-specific legal and compliance review.
