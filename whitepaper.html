Project brief & whitepaper.

A concise, web-native project document designed to be readable, versionable and aligned with the public website.

Document version 3.0 · October 2026. This web whitepaper is informational and should be read together with the transparency and legal pages.

1. Executive summary

AEDT is an independent TRON-based digital asset project designed around AED-linked market positioning, public on-chain identity and utility-led development. The project’s public interface focuses on verifiability: contract, market references, disclosures and ecosystem use cases are surfaced in a consistent structure.

2. Design principles

Identity first. AEDT should be identified by its deployed TRC-20 contract rather than ticker alone. Market transparency. Trading data is presented as a dynamic third-party estimate, not a guaranteed value. Utility before hype. The ecosystem roadmap prioritizes real digital workflows over unsupported price claims.

3. Technical layer

AEDT is presented on TRON using the TRC-20 token standard. The project contract is TV714TT9aQftbkYBjWiVExCxNZm9nPJX6K. Public blockchain infrastructure allows users and reviewers to inspect transfers, holders and token information through independent explorers.

4. Market layer

AEDT’s primary publicly referenced pair is AEDT/USDT. The existence of a USDT pair does not create an affiliation with Tether. Liquidity, volume, depth and price can vary. The website can display public API estimates but cannot guarantee accuracy or execution.

5. AED-linked positioning

The AED-linked narrative is intended to make the project understandable in a UAE-dirham-oriented context. Unless supported by separate enforceable documentation, this framing does not by itself create a bank deposit, legal tender, deposit insurance, reserve guarantee or automatic redemption right.

6. Ecosystem direction

The AEDT roadmap includes merchant and digital-service experiences, website integrations, automation and Web3 infrastructure. MetaOneClick is positioned as a service and execution layer for planned digital experiences such as websites, CRM, AI automation and Web3 launch support.

7. Security

Users should verify the exact contract, protect wallet secrets, confirm transaction destinations and use independent market/explorer references. Future project security improvements may include third-party reviews and versioned technical disclosures where appropriate.

8. Risk factors

Digital assets can involve price, liquidity, smart-contract, operational, cyber, fraud, counterparty, tax and regulatory risks. Public market data may be delayed or inaccurate. Availability in any jurisdiction or platform can change.

9. No investment promise

AEDT materials do not promise profit, capital protection, a future price, guaranteed liquidity, a listing outcome or a risk-free stable value. Users and counterparties should perform independent diligence.
