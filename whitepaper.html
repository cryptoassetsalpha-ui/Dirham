A clear, web-native AEDT project brief.

A concise overview of the token, technical layer, market surface and ecosystem direction.

Document version 4.0 · Light Edition · October 2026.

1. Executive summary

AEDT is an AED-linked digital asset on TRON designed around visible on-chain identity, public market access and utility-led ecosystem development.

2. Design principles

Identity first. The full TRC-20 contract sits at the centre of the project experience. Live visibility. Market and network information are surfaced in real time. Utility-led growth. The roadmap connects AEDT with digital commerce, Web3 services and automation.

3. Technical layer

AEDT uses the TRON network and TRC-20 token standard. The project contract is TV714TT9aQftbkYBjWiVExCxNZm9nPJX6K. On-chain activity can be explored through TRONSCAN.

4. Market layer

The website connects AEDT with live public market feeds for price, liquidity, volume, market movement and historical charting. The primary public pair shown on the website is AEDT / USDT.

5. AED-linked identity

AEDT is built with a clear AED-oriented brand identity, pairing regional relevance with blockchain portability and public network visibility.

6. Ecosystem direction

The AEDT roadmap covers merchant experiences, digital-service integrations, Web3 infrastructure, CRM workflows and AI automation. MetaOneClick serves as the execution layer for selected digital experiences.

7. User experience

The website is designed around simple verification, fast access to the live market, clean contract references, responsive mobile layouts and direct links to public project channels.

8. Growth priorities

Future priorities include richer dashboards, merchant pilots, partner tooling, integration documentation, automation flows, ecosystem campaigns and new utility experiences.

9. Public channels

AEDT maintains public X and Telegram channels, while technical and ecosystem integration enquiries can be routed through MetaOneClick.
